《計畫》將空氣中微小的塵埃、水氣與光線計算入畫，將看不見的大氣與水氣，以數位計量之單位格大小揭露其權重及不可或缺。  
"Computing Drawing" incorporates calculations of dust, moisture and light from the air into the drawing. The invisible atmosphere and water vapor are revealed in digital units, showcasing their importance and indispensability.
