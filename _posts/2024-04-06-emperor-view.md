The artwork indicates a transition from computational aesthetics to computational neuroaesthetics, integrating insights from neuroscience and the study of the brain.
Computational neuroaesthetics investigates the neural and brain mechanisms responsible for visual illusions induced by paintings. "The Emperor's New World" aims to introduce novel neuroaesthetic experiences and techniques based on autostereogram. It challenges viewers' ability to adjust their eye parallax, triggering the biological instinct to perceive three-dimensional depth and recognize hidden paysage, such as "brain new world", embedded within the surface painting.
